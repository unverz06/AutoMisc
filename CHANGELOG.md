## 1.0.0
- Initial release, rebuilt from scratch on the AutoQuests structure
- Options panel (Options > AddOns > AutoMisc) with one checkbox per feature
- Pause keys: hold Shift, Ctrl or Alt to suspend the addon (each key can be turned off)
- Sell grey items at vendors, with a gold summary
- Auto repair: guild funds first, then your own gold (no guild, no allowance left, or guild refusal)
- Optional: accept summons, accept resurrections, decline duels, accept group invites from friends and guild
- `/automisc` or `/am` with `on`, `off`, `config`
